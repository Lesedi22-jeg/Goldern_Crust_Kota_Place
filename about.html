About Golden Crust Kota Place

Golden Crust Kota Place is a South African street food business based in Orlando West, Soweto.

The business was founded by Thabo Nkosi in 2018. What started as a small street food cart grew into a full-fledged store by 2020.

Today, Golden Crust Kota Place is known for its distinctive Crunchy Kota, generous portions and authentic South African street food.

Our History

Golden Crust began in 2018 with the goal of providing delicious and affordable Kota meals to the local community.

Through dedication, quality food and excellent customer service, the business grew from a street food cart into a popular local establishment.

Golden Crust currently serves more than 200 customers each day and continues to grow within the Soweto community.

Our Mission

Our objective is to provide authentic, high-quality South African street food that respects township culture while creating employment opportunities for local youth.

Our Vision

By 2030, we want to establish five Golden Crust locations throughout Gauteng and become one of the most recognisable Kota brands in Johannesburg.

Our Target Audience

Golden Crust Kota Place serves customers between the ages of 18 and 45.

  • Local residents
  • Students
  • Office workers
  • Tourists
  • Families

Our customers are looking for quality South African street food at affordable prices.
